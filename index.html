HOW TO PLAY — FROGGIT: SKY AND ABYSS

1. Hop Hills & Highlands WASD / Arrows

Hop tile-by-tile across 96 winding rows of left/right dogleg paths, rolling hills, elevated highland plateaus, moving ferries, and spring bounce pads. Stay away from unfenced cliff edges and mid-track chasms!

2. Natural Pink Tongue Grapple SPACE / Click

Touching patrolling bugs directly is lethal, but whipping your pink tongue snipes them safely from afar for combo points and mild growth. Strike the Realm Guardian 3 times to shatter its shields!

3. Multi-Axis Obstacles Knockback Physics

Bastion Cannons, Swinging Battle-Axes, Vertical Thwomp Monoliths, Rolling Spiked Logs, and Sweepers physically shove Tode—time your crossing so you aren't knocked into a chasm!

4. Checkpoints & Realm Hoops ENTER ↵

Cross glowing 🚩 Checkpoint overlooks to save your progress (press ENTER on wipeout to revive). Leap into the 🌀 Portal Hoops at the finish shrine to ascend to the 👑 Sun Crown or dive to the 🌊 Mariana Abyss!
